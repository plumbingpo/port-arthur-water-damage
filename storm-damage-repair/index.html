Warning Signs for Storm Damage Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • A basement or ground floor that floods when the drains cannot keep up
  • Shingles that are gone, cracked or curled up when you look from the yard
  • Sagging or detached gutters that spill rainwater down the siding
  • Rooms that start to smell stale or moldy following a storm
  • Damaged or displaced flashing around roof penetrations, valleys or edges
  • Attic or wall insulation that is damp or matted down following a storm
  • Standing water in attic spaces, especially by skylights and chimneys

Storm Damage Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Reseal worn caulk and weatherstripping around windows and doors before the rainy season
  • Know how to shut off your main water supply in case a storm damages a pipe
  • Clean out downspouts and gutters regularly, which lets heavy rainfall drain away as it should
  • Schedule a roof inspection every year so weak spots are repaired before storm season
  • Make sure the attic is properly ventilated and insulated to prevent ice dams
  • Trim trees near your home to lower the risk of wind-driven branch damage

Our Storm Damage Repair Process

Our systematic approach delivers quality results on every project. Here's how we handle storm damage repair from start to finish:

1

Emergency Board-Up & Tarping

We secure your property by tarping roof damage, boarding broken windows and sealing breaches so no more water gets in while permanent repairs are planned.

2

Water Extraction & Assessment

Our team pumps and vacuums out the water that came in with the storm. We then assess all affected areas with moisture meters and thermal imaging to map the full extent of the damage.

3

Drying the Structure

To keep mold from growing, we dry the flooring, wall cavities, insulation and roof decking with commercial air movers and dehumidifiers.

4

Rebuild and Permanent Repairs

Whatever was damaged gets fixed or swapped out, from roofing and siding to drywall, flooring and insulation. When we finish, the building is returned to how it looked before the weather hit.
