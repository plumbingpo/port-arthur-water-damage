Before anything else in a restoration project can work, the water has to be extracted. Structural materials, floors, possessions and building systems all take less damage when standing water comes out sooner. Every hour that water sits on surfaces, it penetrates deeper into materials, soaking through carpet into padding, wicking up drywall, saturating wood framing and seeping into subflooring. Across Port Arthur, Port Arthur Restoration handles emergency extraction with industrial portable and truck-mounted machines that can remove hundreds of gallons a minute.

Truck-mounted units rank with the strongest water removal systems made, and their suction lifts water out of hard floors, carpet and structural materials much better than anything you can rent or buy. For areas truck-mounted hoses cannot reach, we deploy powerful portable extractors, submersible pumps and specialty tools designed for specific situations, including water trapped in wall cavities, between floor layers and in confined spaces.

There is more to a professional extraction than getting rid of the water in plain sight. Water migrates behind baseboards, below flooring, into wall cavities and under cabinets, and our technicians, who are IICRC-certified, locate it with moisture detection tools. The more moisture our specialty tools take from these concealed areas ahead of drying, the shorter and lighter the structural drying job becomes, and the lower the chance of secondary mold growth.

Maintenance Tips for Water Extraction

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Pour water into the sump pit a few times a year to confirm the pump kicks on
  • Make sure your home has working water leak sensors in critical areas
  • Early leak signs show up near appliances, toilets and sink cabinets, so check those spots on a schedule
  • Consider an automatic shutoff valve, which senses a leak and closes the water supply
  • Program (804) 575-2641 into your phone so you can call immediately in an emergency
  • Find the main shutoff valve for the house and put a clear label on it

How to Tell You Need Water Extraction

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Storm water collecting in the lowest level of the home
  • Pooled water on the carpet, or a soggy feel with every step
  • Floors flooded by a fire sprinkler or suppression system going off
  • Stairwells or window wells that fill up and spill water inside
  • Water sitting on a floor anywhere in the building, no matter where it came from
  • An appliance, HVAC unit or pipe that is leaking right now
  • Seams in the floor that ooze water under foot pressure
